| 2026-08-02 | **Review follow-up to the structured-findings landing (same day).** Two defects fixed against the shipped implementation. (1) `lib/extract-findings-json.sh` anchored the strip on the **first** `<!-- FINDINGS_JSON_BEGIN -->` in the chunk body, so a review that merely *quoted* the sentinel — the canonical case, since this repo reviews its own PRs and six of its tracked files contain the literal string — had everything after the quote swallowed as "the block": the review was truncated, the real sidecar was consumed with it and failed to parse, and the surviving markdown could fall under the 200-byte empty-output floor and turn a clean chunk into a fail-closed REQUEST_CHANGES. Reproduced at 597 B → 73 B. Now anchored on the **last complete `begin`…`end` pair** (the prompt requires the block to be last), and an unterminated `begin` is left in place — a fenced JSON block rendered in the body is cosmetic, a truncated review is not. Same defect class as LADR-031: a control channel must not be forgeable by review content. (2) Aggregation's full-coverage precondition counted sidecar **files on disk** (`ls`), so a sidecar rejected at the slurp or as a malformed document still counted as covered and could render a summary silently missing that chunk while reporting complete coverage. `merge-findings.py` now reports `merged_chunks` — the chunks it actually ingested — and aggregation gates on that. (3) `residual_risks` and `testing_gaps` were collected by the merge and rendered nowhere — dead data no human or agent ever saw. They are now deduplicated across chunks (`soft_key`, same normalisation as the title leg of `fingerprint()`) and rendered as 🟡 `Testing gap:` / `Residual risk:` bullets inside `### 🟡 Medium Priority Issues`, **without** a `[VERIFIED]` tag: `eval/lib/score-review.sh` counts a flag only when the label carries the tag *and* a severity keyword, and DR precision is zero-tolerance, so tagging them would let an honest "no test covers the new branch" note fail a must-not-flag fixture. Untagged they still reach the reader and `extract-ai-analyse-scope.sh`. (4) The eval caveat named `GEMINI`/`gemini-3.1-pro-preview` as the pair to re-validate on; the deployed pair is **MiniMax M3**, and the Gemini ids in `OPENCODE_REVIEW_REPORT_MODEL_*` are literal fallbacks rather than what the gate is run with — corrected, so the instruction does not send a reader to validate a pair nobody uses. `test-merge-findings.sh` 61 → 82 assertions (10j–10o, 11b2–11b3, 13a–13i). | LADR-055 |
